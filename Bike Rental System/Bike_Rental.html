How RevRide Works

RevRide is Delhi's fastest-growing bike rental platform. We offer a carefully curated fleet of India's most iconic motorcycles at fair, transparent prices tied directly to the bike's real-world value.

Pricing Logic

Simple rule: the more premium the bike, the higher the daily rental. A Hero Splendor starts at ₹200/day while a KTM Duke 390 goes for ₹899/day. Pricing includes GST and a fully refundable ₹500 security deposit.

UPI Payments

Pay instantly via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, or any UPI-enabled app. Scan our QR code with your phone camera and complete payment securely. Card payments also accepted.

Live Availability

All bikes show real-time availability. Green badge = Ready to ride. Red badge = Currently rented out. Browse alternatives or check back soon!

Secure Payments

We use industry-standard encryption for all transactions. Your payment data is protected with bank-level security. All transactions are verified through a QR-based payment gateway.
